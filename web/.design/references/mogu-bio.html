Nature-Grown. Designed to Perform.

Nature is the best architect of all. Drawing on nature’s most advanced materials and processes, Mogu develops responsible products for interior design.

Kana Panels

KANA panels are bio-based Acoustic Wall Panels for sustainable interior. They are made from a carefully engineered blend of natural and bio-based materials.

The surfaces of Kana acoustic panels can be fully customized to suit specific projects and bespoke configurations.

3D Acoustic Panels

A collection of decorative wall panels with remarkable sound absorption properties – did we already say they are extremely beautiful too?

LEARN MORE

Mogu Acoustic panels are the first commercially available products of their kind, entirely made of fungal mycelium and of upcycled textile residues. Thanks to their unique technology, Mogu Acoustique technologies represents today the most sustainable solution dedicated to acoustic comfort. Discover their exceptional perfomances here!

Pluma Panels

A collection of slim, 100% circular wall covering panels with sound-absorbing properties. They are designed to give an innovative and natural look to all spaces.

LEARN MORE

With our latest innovation, the customization potential of PLUMA panels is boundless. Now, you can imprint your texture onto the mycelium, resulting in a completely personalized and one-of-a-kind product.

Resilient floors

From agro-industrial residues to luxury living environments, Mogu Floor are two collections of bio-based resilient tiles and roll for interior design and architecture. A perfect solution to support a fully circular approach within the building industry.

LEARN MORE

The proprietary formulation of the bio-based resin utilised in Mogu Floor products is literally bio-based, replacing traditional industrial pigments with low-value biomasses, such as corn crops, rice straw, spent coffee grounds, discarded seaweed and clam shells. Despite their look resembling stone, and their structural toughness, Mogu Floor products feature a warm and soft touch - providing an amazing and sustainable choice to walk on.

Our materials and products combine remarkable technical properties with radically innovative aesthetics.

By developing bio-based materials inspired by nature, we create new sensory and spatial experiences that encourage a more conscious relationship with the materials shaping our everyday environments.

From homes and workplaces to retail and hospitality spaces, Mogu envisions interiors where material innovation, beauty and environmental responsibility coexist in harmony.

 

A mind for innovation

Mogu carries on complex and iterative R&D projects, allowing to keep on exploring radically innovative solutions, based on mycelium and bio-based materials. 

By blending scientific precision with design sensitivity, Mogu technologies redefine matter itself to deliver unprecedented products such as acoustic panels, wall coverings, and flooring, for the creation of truly responsible and highly comfortable everyday spaces
